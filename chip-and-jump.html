You have to reach your ball on foot. Fall in and you restart from the last solid ground you stood on. A ball in water or out of bounds costs one stroke. Red springs launch you and your ball. Roll in too fast and it lips out. Wind pushes the ball while it flies, so watch the flag. Each hole hides three gold stars to jump for, and many hide a power-up box: P for a power shot, M for a magnet cup, U for a mulligan. Swirling portals warp you, or your ball, to their twin. Red pinball bumpers bounce your ball away faster. Fans blow you and your ball upward, and a golden key opens a locked gate.
